Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

Business owners: business name, mobile number, a hashed password (we never store your password in plain text), and the settings you enter (services, prices, timings, address).

Customers who book: name, mobile number, the service and the date and time booked, and the text of the chat messages sent to the booking assistant.

How we use it

Only to run bookings: to show bookings to the business you booked with, to prevent double bookings, and to let you sign in. We do not sell personal data and we do not show ads.

AI processing

Chat messages, along with the business's published details, are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate replies. Please do not share sensitive information (such as bank details or passwords) in the chat.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a MongoDB Atlas database. Connections use HTTPS. Each business owner can only see their own bookings.

Deleting your data

To delete your account or a booking, write to REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL from the mobile number you used, and we will remove it.
